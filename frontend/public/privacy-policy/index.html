Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026

This policy explains how QUIET WORK 101 ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website, request a visit, or book a seat, cabin or meeting room with us.

Information we collect

  • Name, email address and phone number you provide when booking a seat, requesting a visit, or contacting us.
  • Booking details such as category, date, time and number of guests.
  • Payment information processed securely by our payment gateway (Razorpay). We do not store card or bank details ourselves.

How we use this information

  • To confirm and manage your booking or visit request.
  • To contact you about your reservation, including by email or phone.
  • To process payments and generate invoices.
  • To improve our services and respond to enquiries.

Sharing of information

We share the minimum information necessary with service providers who help us operate — our payment processor (Razorpay), our email provider for booking notifications, and our accounting/invoicing provider (FreshBooks). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We keep booking and contact records for as long as needed to provide our services, meet accounting and legal obligations, and resolve any disputes.

Your choices

You can ask us to review, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.
